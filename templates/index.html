Listing Description

The address alone earns attention — Mokulua Drive runs along the back edge of Kailua's most sought-after residential stretch, and at 456, the upstairs primary suite frames the Mokulua islands directly. Four bedrooms, three baths, 1,820 square feet organized around the water view and the trade wind cross-breeze that moves through this block off the channel.

The kitchen renovation went all the way: waterfall quartz island, Bosch appliances, a wide pass-through that opens onto the dining lanai without a hard threshold. Hardwood floors run from the living room straight through the sliding glass and across the covered lanai. Three bedrooms sit on the main floor, each with plantation shutters and ceiling fans. Upstairs, the primary suite carries its own bath, a walk-in shower, and that view. A solar PV system handles the HECO bill — monthly charges in the mid-thirties.
